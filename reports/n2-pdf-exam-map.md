## Result and interpretation

The PDF contains **34 question blocks**, not 40. The labels **N2-15, N2-24, N2-27, N2-28, N2-29, and N2-33 do not occur anywhere in the file**. Pages 200–233 and 440–473 are answer-key sections; a complete visual scan of all 473 pages found no hidden question blocks in either section.

No block in this compilation is an untouched official JLPT booklet. The collection repeatedly places material traceable to old-format `2級` tests inside later `N2`-style layouts, and several blocks splice questions from different years. The external distribution page describes the file as **40 practice tests**; the official JLPT FAQ says complete past administrations are not published. The PDF covers also say `200点`, whereas official post-2010 N2 uses a 0–180 total scale. Accordingly:

- **Block identity** and **question provenance** are separate. A date in this report is a dominant question-source session, not the identity or administration date of the complete N2-X block.
- **Official/unofficial** describes the block as printed in this PDF. Every present block is unofficial: either a reconstruction/adaptation, a composite, or a mock test.
- A provenance year/month is mapped only when the match is at least 95% certain. Values from 80–94% are explicitly labeled partial-source possibilities and never become block dates. **Unresolved** means that no single session clears the mapping threshold for the block.
- For 1991–2008, the month is December: the JLPT was held once annually before 2009. In 2009 it was held in July and December, so a bare “2009” source cannot safely be converted to a month.
